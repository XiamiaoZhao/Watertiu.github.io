About

I am a PhD student in the Department of Mathematical Sciences at Tsinghua University, advised by Professor Mei Lu. Before joining Tsinghua, I studied at Nankai University.

My research centers on combinatorics and graph theory. I visited the Alfréd Rényi Institute of Mathematics in Budapest for one year with support from the China Scholarship Council, working with Ervin Győri and Dániel Gerbner.
